# Playbooks

## Use Case

Use this pack when a reviewer needs to know whether oxygen evidence is strong enough to justify follow-on analysis for a real allocation decision.

The first valid use case is narrow:

1. Identify public oxygen need and facility-readiness sources.
2. Classify each source by measure family.
3. Exclude sources that cannot separate equipment presence from functionality.
4. Only then draft a triage memo for procurement, maintenance, financing, or insufficient-evidence buckets.

## Non-Use Cases

- Do not use equipment counts as proof of oxygen access.
- Do not recommend oxygen source types without facility-level demand, power, maintenance, supply-chain, and workforce context.
- Do not compare countries unless numerator, denominator, year, source family, and functionality fields are compatible.
- Do not publish operational recommendations without domain review, red-team review, and replication.

## Field-Reality Questions

- Who is the named user: ministry oxygen lead, hospital director, biomedical engineer, procurement unit, donor, or market-shaping partner?
- What decision could change: procurement, maintenance contract, tariff or bulk purchase, pulse oximeter supply, training, or facility referral planning?
- What timing matters: annual budget, emergency response, procurement cycle, maintenance schedule, or facility assessment?
- What observation would overturn the recommendation?

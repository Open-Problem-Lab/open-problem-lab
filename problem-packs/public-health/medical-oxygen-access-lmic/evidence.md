## Evidence Notes

### 2025 Lancet Global Health Commission on medical oxygen security

Use this source for the strategic case that oxygen is not a single device problem. It is a system problem spanning demand estimation, production, storage, delivery, maintenance, pulse oximetry, training, and financing. Do not use it as proof that a specific country has a specific shortage.

### 2023 WHA76.3 resolution on medical oxygen

Use this source for the decision-maker pathway. The resolution names the institutional levers that ministries and partners can change: planning, procurement, distribution, regulation, maintenance, workforce, and monitoring. It does not prove those levers have moved in any country.

### WHO oxygen source and distribution guidance

Use this source to keep the pack from collapsing oxygen access into equipment presence. Oxygen cylinders, concentrators, PSA plants, liquid oxygen, piping, power, accessories, and maintenance have different failure modes.

### Assistive technology and rabies comparator records

These records exist to make discovery-mode selection accountable. They support the ranking table, not oxygen-specific claims.

## Evidence Quality Rule

An oxygen source is not useful if it only counts devices. A reviewer must be able to tell whether the source measures clinical need, oxygen source availability, source functionality, pulse oximetry, power, consumables, trained staff, cost, or patient outcomes.
